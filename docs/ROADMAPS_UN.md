## The "Arborization" Lifecycle

1. **Discovery**: Subagent identifies a roadmap node (e.g., "OAuth" or "gRPC").
2. **Deepening**: Subagent attaches the corresponding **MCP Server** to acquire the skill.
3. **Multiplication**: Subagent federates the newly acquired skill across the jurisdictional mesh.
